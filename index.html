Skill tree

Outfitter

Hats and pendants cost Prunus and give permanent % buffs while worn. Equip them in the Gear section of the inventory (I). Gear survives prestige. Luck buffs apply to newly revealed tiles.

Pet forge

Add shinies to the forge, then sacrifice 3 Shinies of one ore, or 1 Vitrariant, to roll a pet. Shinies in the forge leave your variant inventory until you take them back. Rarer ores and Vitrariants improve your odds of rare pets. Duplicates level a pet up (+25% per level, max Lv 10). One pet follows you, and a second pet slot unlocks when you unlock the Moon (10 prestiges).

Epic, Legendary, Mythical and Vitradical pets can craft as a Shiny variant: 1 in 10, or 1 in 4 when you sacrifice a Vitrariant. Shiny pets sparkle and have +50% stats. They level up separately from normal pets.

Inventory

Equip up to 4 tools. Equipped slots are hotkeys 1–4. Unequipped charms do not trigger. The extra Charm slot holds one passive charm without using a tool slot.

Gear

Hat, pendant and pet give permanent % buffs. Tap a slot to unequip it.

Upgrades

Prunus shop

Pink Prunus blossoms drop from mined blocks, more often and in larger amounts the deeper you dig. Each charm has a chance to trigger every time you finish mining a block. Prunus and charms survive prestige.

Prestige

Frame shop

Frame Shards come from prestiging in the Vitra Frame. Frame-only charms are sold in the Prunus shop while you are here.

Lunar shop

Lunar Cores come from prestiging on the Moon. These upgrades only exist here.
